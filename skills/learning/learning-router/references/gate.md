# GATE adaptation

Use this reference when preparing GATE material. The method skills also work for other exams and general learning; choose their sources and assessment conventions accordingly.

## Scope and source

Read the current project's AGENTS.md and subject-coverage or equivalent status before assuming a topic is unplanned or unstudied. Use the syllabus year in force and the learner's current paper priorities. Treat schedule targets, acquired books and untaken tests as different from demonstrated progress. Verify dates, registration and exam rules from current official records when needed; this skill set hardcodes none of them.

Use the subject's existing Syll.md, prerequisite plan, study-guide.md and resources.md when available. Inspect the actual passage before giving PDF viewer pages. Books and lectures may be procured; a narrow topic may be taught directly when that is the better use of time. Name a missing source without treating its absence as a permanent restriction.

Use authentic PYQs with checked provenance and keys for exam calibration. Keep year, paper and question identifiers when supplied; leave unknown identifiers unknown. Label original questions and generated variants as practice. Verify their solutions before presentation. Preserve designated unseen papers.

## Match the method to the skill

- Definitions, theorem conditions and formulas: retrieval plus spacing, retaining assumptions and units.
- New calculations or constructions: a complete worked example, omitted steps, then an independent problem.
- Algorithm choice, probability tools and look-alike concepts: interleaving after the foundations are learned.
- Pointer execution, parsing, caches, page replacement and synchronization: prediction plus a checked trace.
- Overgeneralized rules: self-explanation and a valid counterexample, followed by a changed case.
- Paper selection, speed and accuracy: independent timed practice, then analysis of actual losses.

These are local teaching applications, not GATE-specific experimental findings. MCQ, MSQ and NAT practice should follow the paper's verified scoring rules. Marking a derivation for teaching does not imply the exam awards partial credit for that working.

## Preserve repository data

Learning tasks do not authorize changing historical PYQ counts, generated sidecars, coverage tags or calendar targets. When study edits are requested, follow the project's formats and required audit. Reuse existing progress records; tick only what the project's completion convention and actual evidence justify. Ordinary teaching does not require rewriting a study plan.

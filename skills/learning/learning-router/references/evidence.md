# Research basis, reading and limits

Compiled from sources checked in the learning-method research session on 2026-11-15. Links identify the papers or original essays. Some paywalled articles were checked through their abstracts or bibliographic records rather than unrestricted full text. This is a targeted research synthesis, not a systematic literature review or a validation of this whole AI skill set.

## Retrieval and spacing

**Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan and Willingham, 2013.** [Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26173288/), Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14, 4-58. DOI 10.1177/1529100612453266. Broad review of ten methods. Practice testing and distributed practice received high utility ratings. Self-explanation, elaborative interrogation and interleaving received moderate ratings. These are the review's ratings at its publication date, not permanent universal rankings.

**Roediger and Karpicke, 2006.** [Test-enhanced learning: Taking memory tests improves long-term retention](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x), Psychological Science, 17, 249-255. Experiments with prose passages support delayed retention from retrieval relative to repeated study under the tested conditions. They do not establish that recall alone trains every kind of technical problem solving.

**Agarwal, Nunes and Blunt, 2021.** [Retrieval Practice Consistently Benefits Student Learning: a Systematic Review of Applied Research in Schools and Classrooms](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09595-9), Educational Psychology Review, 33, 1409-1453. The classroom review strengthens the case beyond laboratory tasks. Its results are not a GATE-specific effect estimate.

**Cepeda, Pashler, Vul, Wixted and Rohrer, 2006.** [Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks: A review and quantitative synthesis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16719566/), Psychological Bulletin, 132, 354-380. DOI 10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354. Meta-analysis of 317 experiments supports distributed practice and the importance of timing. A fixed day-1/day-3/day-7 schedule is a practical heuristic, not its universal prescription.

Application: retrieve before rereading, correct the gap, then revisit it. Rereading or highlighting may help locate and understand content, but are weak substitutes for an independent check. Spacing schedules the revisits; it does not determine whether the knowledge was understood.

## Examples, explanation and strategy selection

**Chi, Bassok, Lewis, Reimann and Glaser, 1989.** [Self-explanations: How students study and use examples in learning to solve problems](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1), Cognitive Science, 13, 145-182. Analysis of learners studying mechanics examples links explanatory activity with how they learn and use examples. This supports asking why steps work; the observational comparison alone is not a universal causal estimate.

**Renkl, Atkinson, Maier and Staley, 2002.** [From Example Study to Problem Solving: Smooth Transitions Help Learning](https://doi.org/10.1080/00220970209599510), Journal of Experimental Education, 70, 293-315. Field and laboratory work supports fading solution steps as learners move toward solving, especially for near transfer. Backward fading was more favorable in the studied conditions. The skill uses it as an adaptable starting point, not an invariant lesson sequence.

**Rohrer, Dedrick and Stershic, 2015.** [Interleaved Practice Improves Mathematics Learning](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1071568), Journal of Educational Psychology, 107, 900-908. Classroom experiment supports mixing problem types to practise selecting a strategy. This is not evidence that arbitrary subject switching or mixing untaught procedures always helps.

Application: model unfamiliar procedures, explain their decisive steps, reduce assistance and later ask the learner to choose among familiar methods. Transfer must be checked on changed situations; successful recall of one example does not establish it.

## AI tutoring

**Bastani, Bastani, Sungu, Ge, Kabakci and Mariman, 2025.** [Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12232635/), PNAS, 122, e2422633122. DOI 10.1073/pnas.2422633122. A field experiment contrasted an unrestricted GPT interface with a tutor using safeguards, including teacher-designed hints. Supported practice performance and subsequent unaided performance diverged; safeguards largely mitigated the negative unaided effects. This does not prove that withholding every answer is optimal.

**Kestin, Miller, Klales, Milbourne and Ponti, 2025.** [AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning: an RCT introducing a novel research-based design in an authentic educational setting](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40537565/), Scientific Reports, 15, 17458. DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6. A custom tutor outperformed the comparison active-learning lessons in the studied college physics setting. A short intervention and its post-tests do not establish durable GATE readiness or the efficacy of ordinary chatbot conversations.

Application: check technical outputs, tailor assistance to the learner, and separately assess unaided work. Honor explicit requests for explanations or solutions. Assessing how well the learner used AI is a different question from assessing what they can solve alone.

## Practitioner essays

These are useful design advice, not independently validated packages of teaching instructions.

- [The Learning Scientists, Six Strategies for Effective Learning](https://www.learningscientists.org/blog/2016/8/18-1), Megan Smith and Yana Weinstein, 2016. Accessible research translation; the strategies have different evidence bases.
- [Andy Matuschak, How to write good prompts](https://andymatuschak.org/prompts/), 2020. Designing clear retrieval prompts for facts, concepts and procedures. These are memory questions, not LLM prompt tricks.
- [Michael Nielsen, Augmenting Long-term Memory](https://augmentingcognition.com/ltm.html), 2018. An explicit personal account of using Anki with technical material, not a controlled trial.
- [Piotr Wozniak, Twenty rules of formulating knowledge](https://www.supermemo.com/en/blog/twenty-rules-of-formulating-knowledge). Practical card-writing guidance. Retain context and combine atomic recall with whole problems where appropriate.
- [Ethan Mollick, Assigning AI: Seven Ways of Using AI in Class](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/assigning-ai-seven-ways-of-using), 2023. Tutor and coach roles with failure modes. Its model-specific remarks reflect that date.

## Books

- [Make It Stick](https://www.makeitstick.com/), Peter C. Brown, Henry L. Roediger III and Mark A. McDaniel, 2014. Readable synthesis of retrieval, spacing, varied practice and illusions of knowing.
- [How to Solve It](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691164076/how-to-solve-it), George Polya, originally 1945. Problem-solving heuristics, checking and looking back. A classic practice guide, not an RCT.
- [A Mind for Numbers](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/314056/a-mind-for-numbers-by-barbara-oakley-phd/), Barbara Oakley, 2014. Practical technical-study guidance. Its popular explanatory language should not be treated as a measured guarantee.

## Local design choices and attribution

The router, method boundaries, source verification, assistance labels, error records, predict-trace-check sequences, held-out papers and GATE applications are maintained workflow choices. They combine motivated components; this exact combination has not been experimentally validated. Counterexamples are a logical technique; a tested counterexample can refute a universal claim, while exhaustive-looking examples cannot prove it.
